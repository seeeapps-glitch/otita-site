FAQ

How do I cancel Premium?

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions → OTITA. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

Premium disappeared after I changed phones

In the app, open Settings → Get Premium → Restore purchases. Purchases made with the same Apple Account will be restored.

Can I close the app while it's timing?

Yes. The app saves the time you tapped "Fall", so timing continues even if you close it.

Where are my records stored?

Your records, Watch later list, and settings are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.
